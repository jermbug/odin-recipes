Nam Khao

Back to Recipes List

Description

Also known as Crispy Rice Salad, this dish is known for its crunchy texture from deep fried rice balls, with bouncy flavors from fermented pork and fresh herbs.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups jasmine rice
  • 2 tbsp Thai red curry paste
  • 1 tsp ginger (minced)
  • 2 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1 shallot (very finely chopped)
  • 2 kaffir lime leaves (very finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 2 tsp light brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp corn starch
  • Canola oil (for deep frying)

For salad dressing

  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 garlic clove (very finely chopped)
  • 1 stem cilantro (no leaves - finely chopped)
  • 1 thai chili
  • 1/2 cup boiling water

Other salad ingredients

  • 2 Thai Nam sausage (Lao Som Moo sausage)
  • 1/3 cup mint (roughly chopped)
  • 1/3 cup cilantro (roughly chopped)
  • 1/3 cup Thai Basil (roughly chopped)
  • 1/4 cup roasted peanuts (unsalted)
  • 2 fresh shallots (thinly sliced)(soaked in ice water for 5 minutes)
  • 2 tbsp fried shallots
  • 2 spring onions (finely sliced)
  • 3 Thai chilies (finely sliced)
  • Lettuce leaves

Instructions

To make the rice balls

  1. Cook rice. Remove from heat and spread into sheet pan, covering lightly with paper towels.
  2. Once rice is cooled (about 2 hrs), mix in all other rice ball ingredients except the oil.
  3. Wet your hands and form rice balls about 2 inches in diameter until all rice mix is used up. Refrigerate until ready to fry.
  4. In a medium saucepan, heat oil to 160°C/320°F.
  5. Carefully add 5-6 rice balls into the oil and fry for 3-4 minutes each until crisp. Remove and let try on paper towels.

To make the dressing

  • Combine all ingredients except water, then pour in freshly boiled water and whisk. Let cool completely.

Arranging the salad

  1. In a large salad bowl, break the cooled rice balls into pieces.
  2. Toss together all other salad ingredients. Pour a little bit of salad dressing and mix well.
  3. Leave the rest of the salad dressing in a sauce dish to add as desired.
